# Maths Workshop — Batch 1: Data essentials
24 October 2026

## Added
This batch extends the optional Maths Workshop without changing the existing Tools & Tutorials collection or authoring student IA results.

- **31 new definitions** (57 total) for sampling, bias, frequency, quartiles, histograms, two-way categories and proportions, measurement resolution, rounding, reliability and uncertainty.
- **Five new guided method lessons** (eight total):
  1. **Sampling fairly and recognising bias** — population, sampling frame, proportional allocation and honest caveats about nonresponse and self-selection. Links to the existing Google Forms tutorial for collection, *not* random sampling.
  2. **Describe distributions and choose the right diagram** — explicit median-of-halves quartile convention, IQR, outlier flags, frequency density with unequal class widths, matching box-plot and histogram tools.
  3. **Construct and interpret a histogram** — full worked frequency-density example with unequal class widths, two guided comparisons of bar heights and areas, and independent checking.
  4. **Two-way tables and conditional percentages** — correct row and column denominators, percentages versus percentage-point differences, and pivot tables.
  5. **Measurement resolution and sensible uncertainty checks** — correct-rounding intervals, a simple area sensitivity calculation, difference between rounding and actual measurement uncertainty, and caution against inventing precision.
- **Two guided checkpoints** per new lesson, allowing students to progress, review answers and try a separate independent question. All existing Batch 0 lessons remain accessible with their original single guided question.
- **Compact worked tables**, responsive down to narrow phones; tables are fictional and never transferred into actual IA work.
- **Pathway mappings** for the 16 journeys, with greater coverage for relationships, comparing groups, categorical relationships, variation/reliability, image measurement and triangulation.
- **Reuse of existing software guides and native creators**: Forms, Desmos distributions, Sheets two-way tables, reporting precision, Statistical Diagram Studio and Table Studio. Optional creator buttons open the native workspace without treating practice as IA results.
- **Arithmetic, scope and mobile browser tests** for the lessons, including no mutations of student work and correct return to software guides.

## Safety and interpretation
The Workshop never claims that a method appears on any student's exact syllabus, that a convenient sample is representative, that a numerical trend implies cause, that an outlier should automatically be removed, or that a rounding interval proves the full accuracy of a measurement. A student must explain the actual data collected, method selected, mathematical interpretation and meaningful limitations.

## What comes next — Batch 2: Relationships and fitting
Add canonical definitions and guided methods for **Spearman's rank correlation**, **straight-line regression**, **nonlinear regression and fitting**, **residual diagnostics**, and **model comparison and sensitivity**.

Reuse the existing Google Sheets and Desmos tools. Demonstrate *how to choose and justify* a method by examining the actual data and aim, not a preferred curve. Contrast linear association with nonlinear structure, explain tied ranks and regression direction, require interpretation of residual patterns and fitted-model domain, and avoid treating an R² value as automatic evidence that a model is best.

Suggested implementation order: (1) glossary and method mapping, (2) five worked guides, (3) two-stage guided practice and independent tasks, (4) contextual pathway links, (5) mathematical and phone/desktop regression tests.

# Insight Threads — Pass 3: from findings to justified decisions
24 October 2026

## Purpose

The mathematical reasoning chain should be visible when a student chooses what to do after an authentic finding, reopens a linked mathematical stage, and decides whether to conclude. Pathfinder must not select the student's mathematical conclusion or write an IA paragraph for them.

This builds on Pass 1 canonical Sparks and Pass 2 technique-specific interpretation.

## One canonical chain, no new fields

| Part | Existing source | Recall |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genuine result | Mathematical entry result, completed status | Section 4, Sparks and conclusion |
| Interpretation | Local takeaway or interpretation on that entry | Decision thread and linked stage |
| Open question | Next question on that entry; shared first-page fields on older projects | Section 4, C35, next stage and conclusion |
| Justification | Entry next-why; mirrored first-page next-reason | Section 4, C35, next stage and Direction Spark |
| Mathematical follow-up | Exact from-entry identifier on child stage | Original Direction Spark and parent thread |
| Possible stopping point | Existing C60 answer and limitations | Optional conclusion readiness guidance |

### Critical repair

The dedicated C35/C35H/C35P next-question and next-reason fields are now synchronised with the selected genuine mathematical record, not just the first four-part page. When changing from one confirmed result to another, C35 reloads the new source's own notes. Editing a question after a dependent stage exists continues to trigger the existing dependency-review process.

## Student experience

- First and later mathematics Section 4: a compact, collapsed-by-default “Connect my finding to my next decision” disclosure shows the student's own finding, unanswered question and reason, then offers *continuing* and *concluding responsibly* on equal terms.
- C35 What Next: the same canonical reasoning thread appears alongside the original writable question and justification.
- Linked successor: the existing provenance card displays the student's exact recorded question and why it follows, instead of substituting a generic suggested next technique. The parent and child records retain their review warnings.
- Conclusion (C60): an optional mathematical-readiness disclosure asks whether genuine evidence answers the aim within justified conditions. An open question can become a limitation or possible extension rather than mandatory further work.
- Direction Sparks: explicitly linked next stages and their planned/completed/review status are visible. This does not turn a proposed question into a proven result.

## Invariants and safety

1. A draft is never a result, and creating a child stage does not prove the question was answered.
2. A completed output without interpretation is labelled as such rather than presented as a supported conclusion.
3. Pathfinder does not parse free text to infer that the mathematics is sufficient, a result is correct, or an extra technique is required.
4. Outdated links and reviewed mathematical records continue to be flagged.
5. Unrelated stages do not inherit one another's next questions or methods.
6. Student-provided text and stage titles are escaped.
7. No new student-owned fields, project migration, automatic IA paragraphs or assessment scores.
8. Reduce Advice keeps the disclosures accessible but collapsed; validity warnings remain visible when opened.

## Acceptance journeys

Test constrained optimisation -> boundary/feasibility -> justified continuation or scoped conclusion; correlation and model fit -> genuine interpretation or optional validity check; proof -> stated domain and distinction between evidence and proof; stage-to-stage provenance; edits to an ancestor -> dependent review; no false completed results from plans; legacy saved data; safe conclusion if a recent finding needs review.

Pass 4 will address model accuracy and course-scope guidance. Pass 5 covers further presentation refinement and Toolkit polish. Pass 6 completes longer student-journey usability trials.

### Content vs. presentation rules

1. The large general **Before you write** card groups become one small visible reminder and an explicit *See writing tips* control. Existing purple field icons stay visible.
2. The tip strings are *not edited, condensed or paraphrased*: modules retain their originals and a shared adapter reads them for the pop-in and complete supplementary library. Their item counts are regression-tested.
3. The pop-in first prioritises a relevant tip for that field, then offers the **complete group**, with all examples preserved under disclosure controls. The full guide keeps its 14 lessons and adds a separately collapsed section for every group.
4. Route-sensitive first-maths tips are calculated from the current student's plan and labelled as *possible approaches*, not findings.
5. General guidance is hidden behind on-demand help only. Keep visible short mathematical, safety, evidence and task cues even when advice is reduced.
6. On mobile use the existing scrolling bottom-sheet pop-in; on desktop the existing anchored pop-in. Do not change stored IA fields or export schemas.

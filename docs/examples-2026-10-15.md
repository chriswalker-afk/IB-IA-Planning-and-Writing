# Contextual paired examples — 15 October 2026

## Shipped content

The previously reviewed 24-card draft is now a shared, original teaching library. Five additional cards cover a statistical conclusion, theoretical scope, interpretation of correlation, discrete graphs and categorical displays (29 total). Modelled values are fictional and deliberately separated from students' findings. The cooling dataset, fits, RMSE values, threshold time and residual pattern are tested for consistency.

Every card has a less-effective version, a more-effective version, an explanation of what changed and one question for the student's own work. The box/net diagram is a completed original vector illustration, not a production placeholder. Continuous, discrete and categorical graph contrasts are actual SVG figures. Tables are native accessible HTML; selected formulae use MathML. All source text is escaped; public examples do not embed IB scans or private Drive links.

## Selection boundaries

`context.js` reads the actual selected record, including the previous result selected for C35, C96's writing origin and troubleshooting's saved origin. It does not substitute the newest record. A proposed calculation does not become evidence of a pattern. Cached scatter details are used only when their source record matches. Familiar/unfamiliar/mixed explanations use the student's confirmed knowledge status, not an assumed syllabus list.

Examples may always be browsed for learning. A contextual suggestion is not a claim that a technique has been used, a recommendation to add it, a diagnosis of a flaw, a promised mark or teacher approval. Planning is labelled as a preview. Statistical, modelling and theoretical introductions/conclusions are distinct. Bivariate and course-familiarity cards allow scenario comparisons without editing the actual method.

## State, focus and accessibility

The viewer uses a separate native dialog and an ephemeral session. It receives a read-only state getter, with no save callback. It does not change the writing ledger, stage progress, mathematical records, accuracy policies, limitations, routes or backups. Opening does not re-render the main form. Closing restores the triggering control and scroll position. Nested help returns to the underlying drawer; Escape closes the examples first. Filter fields do not bind to project state. Pairs sit side by side on wider screens and stack on phones. Text labels distinguish the pair, and line styles distinguish graph models without relying on colour alone.

## Editorial basis

The proposal drew principles from the supplied IB moderator coversheets and annotations, including Microwave popcorn (introductory aim, model critique and reflection), Volume of a chess pawn (concision, notation and precision), Internal temperature of cookies (tables, graphs, set-out and model fit), Paracetamol tablet optimization (definitions, diagrams, coherent reasoning and contextual conclusions), and lower-performing work illustrating unsupported claims and unexplained calculations. These principles inspired newly authored examples; no original student passages or marks are assigned to these extracts. Full-IA quality must not be inferred from one fragment. Elementary theoretical examples demonstrate communication, not the mathematical scope of an entire IA.

Automated results and screenshots are evidence for the tested synthetic journeys, not certification of classroom outcomes, external IA edits or every assistive technology. No original source Sheet or project-data schema is changed.

# Problem-first "I am stuck" help - 15 October 2026

The global help menu used to send every student, including students in Planning, to a Completing screen dominated by missing-data choices. This update offers help without changing the current page.

## Student experience

- Up to five context-relevant problems initially; "Other problems" and "Something else / I am not sure" remain available.
- 24 focused situations spanning ideas, aims, methods, prediction, evidence, permission, first mathematics, calculations, interpretation, next steps, surprising findings, understanding, proof, accuracy, limitations, writing, coherence, conclusion, feedback, workload and recovery.
- One immediate action, a useful stopping point and an explicit destination. Examples, two alternative routes at most, teacher-question guidance and private action notes are optional.
- A selected mathematical record drives relevant advice. Planning does not inherit an unrelated completed calculation. Results are not fabricated from a technique title or prediction. Unclassified curvature is not treated as monotonic.
- Help opens over Planning or Completing. Closing it leaves the current draft intact; opening a suggested task offers return to the originating page and mathematical record.
- Student-entered action notes remain pending until explicitly marked helpful. No mathematical stage, teacher approval or external IA edit is inferred. Full JSON backups preserve support state. Older help notes remain readable.

## Implementation

`src/help/stuck-catalog.js` provides reviewed editorial advice with source references to the workbook's Getting Unstuck D/S/H/Q rows and related mathematics/writing libraries. `stuck.js` selects contextual advice and destinations. State, explicit actions and rendering are separate small modules. Existing C95 entry points reuse this same support instead of a second, inconsistent help system. The original workbook snapshot, source nodes and routes are unchanged.

Examples are labelled as illustrative ways of thinking, not model paragraphs or fabricated student findings. Actual sources, selected methods, exact versus approximate values, unfamiliar syllabus components and the distinction between project obstacles and mathematical limitations remain explicit.

## Repeatable checks

`npm test` includes 31 new support regressions (194 total). `scripts/stuck-browser.py` adds 15 synthetic desktop/phone journeys to the existing 10 conformance browser journeys. The regular Conformance browser acceptance workflow runs both suites. Screenshots and results are workflow artifacts, not private student data.

The browser suite checks Planning preservation, the original Help menu, targeted work and return, result selection, no invented results, theoretical and permission routes, private-note JSON backup/restore, contextual writing help, keyboard focus, source readiness, feedback links, legacy C95 routes and 360px layout. These tests do not certify every browser, classroom situation or a student's mathematical understanding.

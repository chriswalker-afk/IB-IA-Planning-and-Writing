# Insight Threads — Pass 2: interpreting real mathematics (24 October 2026)

## Goal

Give a student specific mathematical reading support **immediately beside the result they are trying to interpret**. The aid teaches what a confirmed technique's output represents, how to explain it using the student's quantities and aim, what it cannot establish, and one relevant mathematical validity check. It is not a second next-step recommendation engine or an automatic interpretation of the student's numerical answer.

The work builds on Pass 1's canonical Discovery Sparks, method reasons and proposed next-question links.

## What students see

- Section 3 of the first mathematical exploration (C30) and of every substantial mathematical stage (C40/C50) contains a compact, optional **Help interpret: [technique]** disclosure directly before the interpretation box.
- The dedicated mathematical reflection (C34B) and PMCC interpretation screen (C35P) expose the same aid.
- The full aid draws the student's own aim, result and recorded mathematical purpose directly from their canonical state; all text is escaped. It is *not* a generated paragraph.
- Saved Discovery Sparks also include a concise mathematical reading cue, so students can revisit the key meaning and inference boundary later.
- In Reduced advice mode, the native disclosure remains available but stays collapsed. Existing assessment and Field Guide bubbles are untouched.

## Method coverage

Every selectable specific method from METHOD_OPTIONS receives a dedicated interpretation with mathematical meaning, contextualisation, inference boundary and a check. Coverage includes:

- Pearson and Spearman association; linear, polynomial, exponential, sinusoidal and power modelling
- Optimisation; measured geometry; normal and binomial models; two-sample and chi-squared tests
- Conditional probability, expected value, Voronoi, triangulation and numerical integration
- Rates from data, four financial modelling variants, proof and algebraic derivations

An explicitly recorded scatter diagram receives scatter-specific advice without presuming that correlation or regression has already happened.

## Route coverage and honest fallbacks

The 16 editorial investigative journeys provide route-specific reading questions only where the student has not recorded a more specific mathematical method. These are labelled *planned-route context*, not proof that the named method was performed.

An explicitly chosen "Other" method gets cautious general mathematical reasoning advice: the software must not guess a technique by searching the title or the result text. A confirmed method takes precedence over an old planned route.

## Evidence and safety of interpretations

- No advice is offered as if a plan were a completed calculation: the current entry must be marked completed with a genuine result recorded by the student.
- The system never extracts numerical values, statistics, fitted coefficients, confidence intervals or proofs from prose; it asks the student to interpret their own.
- A student-selected outcome may refine the validity question, but the system never chooses that outcome for them.
- If the entry is flagged as needing review, the aid makes the review explicit and suppresses outcome-specific assertions until the student verifies their work.
- All input remains in existing entry / Planning fields; no new project-state version, migration, stored answers or draft-IA text is introduced.
- This content teaches mathematical inference and is distinct from the Writer's Field Guide (expression), Criteria bubbles (assessment) and I am stuck (recovering from a blocker).

## Regression acceptance

The new tests cover every selectable mathematical technique, all 16 routes, method identity over an earlier route, refusal to infer from free text, completed-vs-planned result discipline, student-selected outcomes, change/review flags, original input safety, reduced advice, saved Spark parity, the existing real writing screens and the original interpretation fields.

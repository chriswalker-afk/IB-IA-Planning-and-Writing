## Second interaction pass — 17 October 2026

This pass makes the earlier Living Journey design interactive rather than merely illustrative. Each explicitly recorded source/accuracy/result dependency can now produce a coloured path line on the Discovery Trail; the student can choose a thread to inspect the saved original statement and follow a real linked mathematical record. The thread explorer is collapsed by default, so the map remains usable even for long IAs. Nothing is inferred from adjacency or from a planned method.

Completed mathematical work that records genuine independent understanding of a new or mixed-syllabus technique becomes a new **Mathematical Learning** spark, which can return at conclusion. Confirmed limitations and learning are kept distinct from provisional advice or invented data.

Constellation stars now have invisible-but-focusable/tappable targets over the plotted coordinates, in addition to accessible record buttons below the diagram. This lets students navigate to the original mathematical record with touch or keyboard.

On normal Planning and Studio tasks, spark notices come after primary student fields rather than ahead of them. Live recorded-state refresh updates the spark after the user edits relevant fields without rerendering the input or moving the typing focus. The returning homepage now draws its welcome context from the last authentic checkpoint or latest real result, and the Studio dashboard highlights one useful focus instead of a completion quota.

All visual components are derived read-only from student-owned state. There are still no grades, required calculation counts, invented mathematical links or automatic IA document edits. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative thread/celebration animations.
